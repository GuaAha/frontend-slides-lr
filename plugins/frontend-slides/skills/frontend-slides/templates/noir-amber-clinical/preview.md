## Visual Snapshot

Noir Amber Clinical opens on a deep black field with a 75px white Chinese promise and one warm amber result. A transparent applicator, premium pack, or dark product monument rises from a controlled copper glow. The composition is luxurious because the product surface, liquid, and typography are exact—not because the page contains ornament. White documentary wells interrupt the black field for scalp maps, matched before/after portraits, reports, or patents. Amber returns in a single progression curve, active-material phrase, or endpoint metric.

The full narrative behaves like a specialist proof dossier. It establishes the root mechanism, shows where to inspect or apply, traces change over time, separates visual evidence from participant evaluation, introduces active materials, demonstrates precision delivery, and ends with use technique and advisory copy. Fine-print sources remain visible. No card shadows, glass UI, gold borders, or medical theater are added.

## International / CJK Preview Note

Chinese carries the narrative. Use explicit semantic line breaks and keep asterisks, percentages, week labels, and scientific abbreviations attached to their claims. MAKE SENSE and HYQiHei are source-specific and not bundled; fallback metrics require re-composition.

## Text Richness Standard

A hero uses one quantified or time-bound promise, one qualification line, and one source marker. A mechanism page supports two linked causes plus one interpreted consequence. A progression page may contain matched evidence, a restrained curve, endpoint interpretation, and a complete method note. Instruction pages use one action per frame and move detailed precautions to the advisory component. The dark field must never be mistaken for permission to hide qualifiers.
