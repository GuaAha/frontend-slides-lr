## Fixed Stage Contract

Keep all authored copy, arrows, evidence labels, and source notes within x=60–690 and y=120–1260. Product photography may bleed or extend beyond the lower edge when the crop is intentional.

This system is premium because it makes physical design legible. Black glass, a warm rim light, and restrained gold create desire; exploded views, hand gestures, and white engineering pages create trust. Neither half is complete alone.

## Overview

The audited source tells a styling-product story through market authority, dark product theater, performance factors, tactile use, scent, washability, refill engineering, magnetic closure, selection guidance, and system pairing. Its reusable lesson is an alternation:

- dark pages establish object value and material presence;
- white pages explain how the object works;
- dark pages return for sensory or styling results;
- white pages close with choice and operation.

Recommended narrative:

1. Optional market or category signal.
2. Black-glass hero with one functional promise.
3. Two-factor performance explanation.
4. Texture and hand-use demonstration.
5. Sensory material or scent chapter.
6. Washability or reversibility proof.
7. Exploded package architecture.
8. Magnetic, refill, or closure sequence.
9. Selector by intended result.
10. System pairing or next action.

Market status is optional and must never lead when the data is weak. If the engineering story is the differentiator, open directly with the hero and move to the exploded view by page three.

## Visual DNA

### Black glass

Dark pages are not flat black. Build a carbon base with a controlled amber or champagne light falling across a translucent, smoked, or glossy object. Maintain real black at an edge so the silhouette has contrast. Avoid filling the entire background with orange haze.

### Engineering white

Technical pages use warm paper rather than cold gray. Headlines are black; diagrams use graphite and thin neutral rules. Gold appears only at a point of innovation or to connect a component to its function. The clean page should feel like a product-design review, not an e-commerce specification sheet.

### Dual rhythm

Use dark/light/dark/light deliberately. Two consecutive dark pages are acceptable when one is hero and one is sensory; three usually flatten the story. White technical pages should not be treated as less important—they often carry the decisive logic.

## Color System

### Roles

- `carbon #080909` is the main dark canvas.
- `glass_black #151616` supports subtle planes and translucent pack forms.
- `paper #F8F8F5` is the engineering canvas.
- `white #FFFFFF` is high-contrast type and reflective detail.
- `graphite #666762` is secondary explanation.
- `line #C9C9C2` is for technical rules and diagram architecture.
- `champagne #D7B56D` is the premium highlight and innovation pointer.
- `molten_gold #B9852E` creates deeper material warmth in photography.
- `cool_outline #6172BE` is a rare comparison or selection cue, never a general accent.

### Page ratios

A dark page uses approximately 68% carbon, 22% product/material image, 8% white type, and 2% gold emphasis. A technical page uses 78% paper, 12% diagram or photography, 8% black type, and under 2% gold or cool outline.

### Contrast

White and champagne work on carbon, but small gold text does not. Use champagne for lines, large numbers, or short labels; use white for paragraphs. On paper, use black or graphite for copy. The cool outline may highlight a selected option but must be paired with a written recommendation.

## Typography

The source’s display faces are not packaged. Recreate its authority through dense weight, compact lines, and generous negative space.

### Scale

- Headline: 75px for hero, section claim, market number, and primary proof.
- Subheadline: 45px for engineering titles and strong support lines.
- Label: 45px for feature labels and prominent diagram annotations.
- Description: 30px for body copy, qualifiers, and concise explanations.
- Disclaimer: 15px for small diagram labels, evidence notes, sources, and legal copy.

Every Chinese role uses 100% line height and -5% letter spacing. Metrics and proof numerals use the same five levels rather than introducing oversized exceptions.

### Dark-page type

Use short white lines and one champagne phrase or index. Left alignment is preferred unless the product is perfectly centered. Do not add multiple award-like labels above a large heading. Award or certification marks belong in a separate rail with provenance.

### White-page type

Technical headings can be more compact and direct: component / action / user value. A small English material word may sit above a Chinese heading but never replace it. Use tabular numerals for measurements when available.

## Layout System

### Grid

Use 6 columns inside the 60 px horizontal safe margins with 15–18 px gutters. Dark heroes often use a 2/4 or 3/3 split, with product mass occupying the wider side. Technical pages use a 4-column diagram field plus a 2-column annotation field, or a centered object with symmetric labels.

### Vertical pacing

- Header/claim: y=120–320
- Object or diagram: y=338–1010
- Method/source: y=1080–1260

An exploded package view may extend from y=260 to y=1080. A market-signal opener may place its large number at y=300–650 with very little else.

### Negative space

Treat emptiness as a material. On dark pages, leave one side nearly black. On technical pages, leave at least 28 px around each exploded component and 20 px around label clusters. Never fill gaps with ornamental specs.

## Component Specifications

### MarketSignal

An optional 500–650 px-high opener centered on one verified category number or rank. Pair the display number with a plain-language scope, period, geography, and source. Do not combine rank, award, sales count, and testimonial in one signal.

### BlackGlassHero

Use carbon with a single warm light direction. Position a glossy or transparent product object at 40–60% of stage height. The claim sits in a calm black region and should describe both action and finish. A subtle gold rim can separate the object; the object must not look cut out by an outer glow.

### AwardRail

Use two or three monochrome marks with equal optical height, followed by one provenance line. If rights-approved logos are unavailable, use text-only recognition labels. Awards support the hero; they do not become the hero.

### FactorPair

Explain two complementary performance factors as a controlled split. Each side contains a short factor name, one mechanism sentence, and one user-facing result. A shared arrow or central material specimen shows cooperation. Avoid molecule theater unless the chemistry is accurate.

### TextureSpecimen

Show one macro surface or swipe on a clean field. Add three properties at most—such as spread, weight, and finish—with a consistent label pattern. Use a hand only if it demonstrates scale or behavior.

### GestureSequence

Use three frames: take / activate / finish, or pick up / distribute / shape. Hands remain consistent in scale and lighting. Each frame has one verb. The sequence is operational, not a lifestyle montage.

### ScentMaterial

Return to dark with cedar, mineral, resin, smoke, or polished wood imagery. The material image occupies 45–70% of the stage. Name only meaningful notes and connect them to the intended mood. Keep fragrance narrative separate from styling-performance evidence.

### WashabilityProof

Use a clear before/rinse/after sequence or a concise test ledger. State water condition, wash step, and observation if known. Avoid using a splash photograph as the only proof. A light page is generally clearer.

### ExplodedPackage

Place the assembled object on one axis and separate cap, housing, refill, base, and closure vertically or diagonally. Use 1.5–2 px leaders, numbered parts, and short function labels. Preserve plausible geometry and consistent perspective. Highlight only the true innovation point in gold.

### MagneticClosureDemo

Show open, attraction, and closed states. One arc or directional line may visualize force; label it as schematic. The closed state must visibly demonstrate alignment or seal. Avoid cartoon magnet icons when the package movement can explain itself.

### RefillSequence

Use 3–4 steps with the same object scale: open, remove, insert, close. Put safety or orientation notes at the step where they matter. A refill benefit belongs after the operation, not as a detached sustainability slogan.

### StyleSelector

Move fully into the paper-white system. Compare up to four intended finishes or scenarios with consistent portraits or hair crops. Use thin rules and one cool-outline selection cue. Rows may cover hold, shine, texture, length, and skill level. Do not rank incomparable products with a single score.

### SystemPairing

Show two products or methods as a sequence rather than a bundle shot. Explain the job of each stage and the combined outcome. Any duration claim remains qualified and sourced. End with a decision or next action, not repeated premium copy.

### EngineeringNote

Use the 15px disclaimer level for a source or schematic note. Keep it within the fixed safe area and directly adjacent to the diagram it qualifies.

## Page Blueprint Library

Use these recipes to translate long product-detail sections into complete fixed stages. A blueprint is selected by communication job, never because an empty slot needs variety.

### G01 — Authority Signal

- One market, category, or milestone statement dominates the page.
- Scope, territory, date range, and source sit directly beneath it.
- Product is a small anchor rather than a monument.
- Black or paper background may be used, but gold remains minimal.
- Remove the page when authority cannot survive independent review.

### G02 — Object Monument

- A short action-and-finish claim occupies the calm black region.
- One transparent or glossy object crosses the amber light field.
- Material edges, closure seams, and surfaces remain recognizable.
- Award marks, if cleared, align in one compact provenance rail.
- Do not add feature callouts around the hero object.

### G03 — Performance Pair

- Two factors share one central material or behavior specimen.
- Each side names role, pathway, and user-facing consequence.
- A central conjunction explains why both are needed.
- Evidence status is shown separately for each factor.
- A factor name never becomes a decorative pseudo-technology badge.

### G04 — Hand and Texture

- Three matched frames show take, activate, and finish.
- Hand position and product scale remain consistent.
- One property is described beneath each frame.
- Macro specimen and finished result may share the final frame.
- A scent label belongs on a separate material pause.

### G05 — Reversibility Proof

- State what is being removed or reversed.
- Show before, process, and after under controlled conditions.
- Record water, action, timing, or number of passes.
- Use paper white and black type for maximum legibility.
- Keep the conclusion proportional to the demonstrated conditions.

### G06 — Exploded Intelligence

- Begin with the assembled object as the orientation anchor.
- Separate parts along a physically plausible axis.
- Number only components discussed in the copy.
- Gold points to the innovation; graphite explains supporting parts.
- Include a schematic note and avoid invented tolerances.

### G07 — Closure or Refill Operation

- Use three to four identical-scale object states.
- Put action verbs above or below a consistent baseline.
- Orientation and safety information sit in the exact relevant state.
- The final state confirms correct alignment or closure.
- State the benefit only after the operating sequence is clear.

### G08 — Selection Matrix

- Define the user decision in the headline.
- Compare up to four options and five useful criteria.
- Keep portraits, objects, and labels on shared baselines.
- Use one pale or cool-outline recommendation cue.
- End with an explicit if/then choice rule.

## Copy Architecture

### Object claim

Use `[physical action] + [visible or felt finish]`. The sentence should describe something the image can support. Abstract claims such as “redefine the future” are incompatible with this system unless followed by a concrete engineering explanation.

### Engineering callout

Use `[part name] — [mechanical role] — [user value]`. Keep each callout under two lines. When the role is inferred from the source rather than verified in technical documentation, label it as an interpretation.

### Status line

Use `[result or rank], [defined category], [territory], [period]`, followed by the source. Do not enlarge the number until all four fields exist.

### Operating instruction

Begin with a verb, then state orientation or force, then the visible completion cue. Example structure: “对准卡位，垂直压入，确认边缘齐平.” This is a grammar pattern, not product instruction.

### Selection rule

Use `Choose [option] when [condition] matters more than [trade-off]`. This makes the matrix useful and prevents all variants from sounding equally ideal.

## Content and Evidence Logic

The system distinguishes status, performance, experience, physical design, and selection:

- Status requires scope, time, geography, and source.
- Performance requires a metric, test, comparison, or clearly labeled qualitative observation.
- Experience may describe texture, scent, finish, and ease of use without masquerading as lab evidence.
- Physical design requires accurate geometry and an operating sequence.
- Selection requires a user condition and an explicit criterion.

Every page should declare which of these jobs it performs. If a page performs more than two, split it.

## Image Direction

Preferred dark assets: smoked glass, glossy polymer, black resin, amber rim light, compact hand gestures, controlled cedar or mineral still life. Preferred light assets: orthographic pack shots, white-background cutouts, component macro shots, consistent hairstyle or use-result portraits.

Retouch product edges carefully. Transparent black objects need both a bright rim and an internal dark mass. Preserve real reflections and material seams. Avoid warm stock-photo gradients unrelated to product geometry.

## Depth and Effects

Depth comes primarily from photographed transparency and light. Use the glass shadow sparingly beneath a major object. Gold rim glow is local, never a page-wide aura. White pages use almost no shadow; exploded components can use a subtle 8–16 px soft lift only if separation is otherwise unclear.

Avoid bevels, metal gradients on type, fake lens flares, dust overlays, and excessive bloom. Premium does not mean noisy cinematic post-processing.

## Shapes and Diagrams

Geometry comes from cylindrical parts, thin leader lines, section cuts, and flat comparison rules. Every authored container, annotation, crop, and control uses square zero-radius corners. Use arrowheads only for motion or assembly; use simple leaders for labels. Circular object details may appear only as intrinsic diagram geometry, never as rounded UI chrome.

## Responsive, Presenter, Static Composition, and Print

The shell centers and uniformly scales the `750×1320` stage. On narrow devices, scroll between complete stages. Presenter notes and controls remain outside the canvas.

Use separate fixed frames for open, attraction, closed, exploded, and assembled states. Schematic motion lines may explain physical force or assembly behavior, but each frame remains complete and simultaneously inspectable. Establish sequence through placement, numbering, leaders, and consistent object orientation.

For export, inspect black detail on the target display, confirm gold does not clip into yellow, and make thin technical lines at least 1.5 px at source size. Test transparent product edges on both RGB screens and PDF output. Keep source notes at the 15px disclaimer level.

## CJK Guidance

Chinese hero lines should be compact and physical: action / finish, object / result, or operation / benefit. Avoid stacking abstract four-character phrases without syntax. Keep technical terms consistent across diagram labels and body copy. Attach numerals to units and use Chinese punctuation in Chinese paragraphs. English material or technology names are subordinate metadata.

## Iteration Guide

First build six pages: hero, factor pair, tactile sequence, exploded package, selector, close. Mark each page dark or light and read the pattern aloud. If the deck clusters too many dark pages, move explanation onto paper. If it clusters too many white pages, add one material or sensory pause rather than coloring every diagram.

Run three reviews. In the object review, verify geometry, light, and material. In the evidence review, verify status, tests, and qualifiers. In the decision review, confirm each comparison leads to a usable choice. Only then tune gold intensity and static frame balance.
