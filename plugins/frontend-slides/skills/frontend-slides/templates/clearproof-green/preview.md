## Visual Snapshot

Clearproof Green feels like a science-minded product editor turned a complete commerce page into a vertical presentation. A 75px black Chinese claim begins at a dependable 60px left rail and usually breaks into two semantic lines. One result-bearing phrase or number turns functional green; everything else stays black, white, or gray. Below the claim, the slide commits to one visual role: a pristine bottle floating in wet material, a four-image problem grid, a thin black-and-green chart, a tightly matched before/after pair, translucent ingredient orbs, a botanical macro, a packaging detail, or a three-step line illustration. The page is flat, square-edged, and unshadowed. Whitespace, crop, rule weight, and typographic scale create hierarchy.

Across a full deck, the rhythm reads as a proof chain rather than a gallery of interchangeable cards. The story opens with a quantified promise, names the user problem, summarizes benefits, explains mechanism, presents measured and documentary proof, pauses for sensory experience, establishes safety or patent trust, teaches use, shows real-life contexts and compatible products, then closes with objections. Full-width black or green bands appear only when the content calls for a material label or a three-part sensory sequence. Fine-print sources remain visible at the bottom. The outcome is clean but not empty, premium but not ornamental, and commercial without surrendering evidence discipline.

## International / CJK Preview Note

The source is Chinese-first. Set simplified Chinese as real editorial copy, add deliberate line breaks, and keep Latin as a compact utility layer (`STEP`, `TYPE`, `BEFORE`, `AFTER`, `Q&A`). MAKE SENSE is a source-specific brand font and is not bundled; fallback metrics can change title width, so every headline must be rechecked. Do not shrink body or legal copy to preserve a Chinese composition. Re-edit or continue the content on another 750×1320 slide.
