## Preview Ingredients

### Page 01 — Mineral hero

- Deep navy to ice gradient.
- One 75px white claim in the dark region.
- One pale blue product object at 40–55% of stage height.
- One powder or clay specimen.
- No icon row or chart.

### Page 02 — Tension and overview

- One respectful portrait or neutral skin/surface context.
- Cleanse-versus-comfort tension expressed in two short statements.
- Four benefits mapped into a blue or mist-blue 2×2 band.
- Labels identify removal, feel, moisture, and texture roles.

### Page 03 — Cleansing mechanism

- Contact / capture / lift / rinse.
- Mineral-blue route and restrained amber residue specimen.
- Plausible layers, simple arrows, visible schematic note.
- No impossible pore claims.

### Page 04 — Hydration orbit

- Central product, skin, or water field.
- Three or four clear, blue, or amber spheres.
- Non-crossing leaders, one role per sphere.
- Ingredient presence stays separate from outcome evidence.

### Page 05 — Texture sequence

- Blue-clay smear plus product context.
- Matched product / foam / rinse macro states.
- Two or three properties only.
- Decorative bubbles do not count as evidence.

### Page 06 — Removal evidence

- Matched surface or swatch images.
- State applied product, amount/method, water condition, and passes.
- One result and one interpretation.
- Method and source remain readable.

### Page 07 — Three-step use

- Amount / emulsify / cleanse-and-rinse.
- One verb and one clear image per frame.
- Quantity, water condition, and caution on the relevant step.
- Close with an operational reminder, not a new claim.

## Text Richness Standard

A normal page carries one headline, one or two explanatory sentences, up to four short labels, and a method/source note when applicable. Aim for roughly 60–90 Chinese characters outside of evidence metadata. The reader should always know whether a statement describes mechanism, observed result, ingredient role, sensory feel, or instruction.

## Do / Don't Preview

**Do:** balance removal and comfort; use one blue phrase per editorial heading; keep mechanisms plausible; label spheres by role; match macro and comparison conditions; disclose removal method; respect real skin texture; replace all source-specific content.

**Don't:** build a generic blue UI; gradient every page; treat foam or pigment as proof; stigmatize skin; draw impossible pores; scatter decorative bubbles; omit comparison conditions; reflow the fixed stage.

## CJK Note

Break headlines by cleanse/comfort tension and keep negation with its verb. Attach numbers to units. Use explicit vocabulary for removal, moisture, feel, and use. The source display font is not bundled.
