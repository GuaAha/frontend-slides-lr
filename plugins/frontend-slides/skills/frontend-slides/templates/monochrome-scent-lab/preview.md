## Fixed-Brand Preview Override

The palette declared in this preview is the color authority for this option. Use its declared `light` or `dark` tone. Keep authored copy inside x=60–690 and y=120–1260. Chinese text uses only 75/45/45/30/15px, 100% line height, and -5% letter spacing. Every authored webpage layer and image mask uses `border-radius: 0`. Product-detail pages have no separately authored brand-logo layer. CSS visuals are allowed when selected; authorized images may be processed and rectangularly cropped while originals are preserved.

## Files

## Selection Metadata

**Best for:** fragrance-led products, paired variants, style-plus-care propositions, editorial product launches, sensory material stories, pump or dose demonstrations, and decks that need legal or proof content without losing visual drama.

**Avoid for:** playful children’s content, colorful community programs, highly technical engineering assemblies, or subjects with no meaningful sensory or variant contrast.

**Source character:** a 750×18305 product-detail board with 16 sections, a black/white hero, matched finish imagery, two opposed fragrance material worlds, function-plus-moisture logic, cream and pump demonstration, selection pages, brand proof, and a closing integrity notice.

**Fixed output:** each page is `750×1320`; the long source board is edited into separate arguments rather than compressed.

## Preview Ingredients

### Page 01 — Split-tone hero

- Black-to-white or black-to-gray field.
- One 75px claim fully inside one tonal zone.
- One or two product variants placed at the boundary.
- Optional category signal only with scope and source.

### Page 02 — Attainable transformation

- Current state, simple intervention, attainable result.
- One matched portrait set or one clear use gesture.
- Explicit skill level and time where relevant.
- No exaggerated frustration collage.

### Page 03 — Finish comparison

- Same subject, angle, crop, and lighting.
- Labels for shine, texture, volume, or definition.
- Visible note if the comparison is illustrative.
- Keep claims separate from visual impression.

### Page 04 — Dual scent world

- Two equal material panels.
- Cool jade/mineral/water world and warm wood/amber/resin world.
- Name, 2–4 notes, and one mood sentence per world.
- Parallel structure, not two unrelated mood boards.

### Page 05 — Duration and function

- Timeline or ledger for persistence, with assessment method.
- Separate styling and care roles around one central specimen.
- One outcome per role.
- Ingredients remain supporting metadata.

### Page 06 — Pump and use

- Black title band, large light operation image, quiet note footer.
- Show lock/unlock, press, and dose when relevant.
- Three or four use steps with one verb each.
- Put quantity and hair/product condition on the correct step.

### Page 07 — Choice and integrity

- Product or variant matrix with up to five criteria.
- One restrained recommendation field.
- Cleared proof strip only.
- Dedicated readable area for warnings, authenticity, or legal instructions.

## Text Richness Standard

Atmospheric pages contain one short claim, a scent name, two to four note labels, and one mood sentence. Functional pages support 60–100 Chinese characters across explanation, labels, and source notes. The page must visibly separate function, sensory association, and evidence. Legal or integrity content is not reduced merely to protect aesthetics.

## Do / Don't Preview

**Do:** keep text within a clear tonal zone; pair scent worlds structurally; use distinct real materials; disclose duration method; match before/after capture; show pump dose; reserve space for integrity guidance; replace all source content.

**Don't:** write across the tonal seam; default to cyan/red neon; fabricate variants by recoloring one image; treat poetry as evidence; use unmatched comparisons; hide qualifiers or warnings; compress 16 sections onto one stage; reflow the fixed canvas.

## CJK Note

Use compact functional Chinese and meaning-based line breaks. Balance paired scent names optically. Keep numbers attached to units and bilingual scent terms subordinate. The source display face is not packaged.

## Preview Rules

1. Every page performs a clear function, sensory, evidence, operation, or decision job.
2. Cool and warm scent worlds have equal structure but genuinely different material imagery.
3. Duration, rank, and before/after claims retain method and qualifiers.
4. Spectrum cyan/red is a rare micro-accent, never a default gradient.
5. The complete `750×1320` stage scales uniformly.
6. Source font, products, portraits, claims, proof marks, and legal text are not reusable by default.
