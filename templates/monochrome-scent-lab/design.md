## Fixed Stage Contract

The internal template is fixed at `750×1320`. It scales as one composition and never reflows its internal grid. All authored copy remains inside x=60–690 and y=120–1260. Full-bleed material or fragrance imagery may reach the edges, but essential labels, duration claims, and legal notes may not.

The system’s identity is a conversation between opposites: black and white, styling and care, ocean mineral and warm wood, atmosphere and measured use. Accent color appears inside a material world, not as a universal brand coating.

## Overview

The audited board is unusually long and narratively broad. It opens with market status and a high-contrast product hero, introduces beginner-friendly styling and finish comparison, enters two distinct scent worlds, returns to functional formulation and cream texture, demonstrates pump dosing and use, presents washability and selection pages, and ends with brand proof plus an integrity notice.

The reusable story should be edited into these chapters:

1. Optional category signal.
2. Split-tone hero: one action, one finish, one product pair or variant.
3. Beginner path or problem-to-result transformation.
4. Honest before/after finish comparison.
5. Dual scent worlds with distinct materials and note families.
6. Duration or experience evidence.
7. Function/care factor pair.
8. Texture and dose.
9. Three-step use.
10. Washability or removal.
11. Product matrix and recommendation.
12. Brand proof and required integrity/legal note.

Not every deck needs all 12. A fragrance-led launch can use 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, and 12. A training deck can emphasize 3, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11.

## Visual DNA

### Split-tone authority

The hero uses a black upper or side field and a white or gray lower field, allowing white and black product variants to coexist. The boundary can be hard, softly graded, or created by photography. The type must belong clearly to one side; do not strand it on the transition.

### Material diptych

The signature content page stacks or pairs two large material environments. One is cool, mineral, jade, water, or mist; the other is warm, wood, amber, resin, or shadow. Each has its own type color and note set. Their composition, scale, and label system remain parallel so the contrast reads as intentional.

### Laboratory return

After scent atmosphere, the system returns to black/white clarity for formula, pump, dose, use, and proof. This return prevents the deck from becoming a mood board and makes functional information easier to trust.

## Color System

### Neutral foundation

- `black #050505` is the highest-contrast field.
- `charcoal #1A1A1A` supports black product detail and secondary panels.
- `white #FFFFFF` is hero text and a clean page field.
- `porcelain #F3F3F1` softens technical pages and image backgrounds.
- `gray #777777` handles secondary explanation.
- `rule #CFCFCB` structures comparisons and matrices.

### Scent worlds

- Cool world: jade `#B9C8C3` and midnight `#001845`, with gray mineral imagery.
- Warm world: amber `#94724A` and amber_light `#EFBF87`, with deep wood or resin imagery.
- Spectrum accents `#16F4EA` and `#F90551` are permitted only as tiny optical or variant cues. They must never form a loud cyber gradient behind text.

### Ratios

A normal neutral page uses at least 80% black/white/porcelain. A material-world page may devote 75–100% to photography, but its text system stays simple. Across the whole deck, cyan/red spectrum accents should remain under 1% of visual area.

### Contrast pairs

Use white on black or midnight, black on white/porcelain/jade, and white on deep amber or dark wood. Test type directly on photography; add a local gradient veil only where needed. Small amber text on white and small jade text on white are not acceptable.

## Typography

The source display type is reference-only. The internal version relies on a strong sans-serif for function and an optional restrained serif for scent-note or material labels.

### Scale

- Headline: 75px for hero, section claim, category number, and primary proof.
- Subheadline: 45px for a scent-world name or strong support line.
- Label: 45px for card titles, step titles, and prominent annotations.
- Description: 30px for body copy, qualifiers, and concise explanations.
- Disclaimer: 15px for note labels, sources, and legal copy.

Every Chinese role uses 100% line height and -5% letter spacing. Metrics and proof numerals use the same five levels rather than introducing oversized exceptions.

### Voice through type

Functional copy is direct sans-serif. Scent prose may use one serif line or lighter sans, but it must remain concrete. Avoid placing long poetic paragraphs in hairline text. A scent world needs a name, 2–4 notes, and one mood sentence—not a novella.

### CJK hierarchy

Use weight, family, color, and placement to create Chinese hierarchy while keeping every role at -5% letter spacing. Break hero copy into action / result or category / differentiator. Keep paired scent names optically equal even when character counts differ.

## Layout System

### Grid

Use 6 columns inside x=60–690 with 14–18 px gutters. Heroes may use a centered 3/3 balance or a 2/4 split. Technical and matrix pages use three paired columns or two broad fields. Scent diptychs usually use two stacked material panels of roughly 430–500 px each plus a title and transition zone.

### Stage zones

- Identity and primary claim: y=120–330
- Material, comparison, or operation: y=340–1050
- Evidence/legal: y=1080–1260

On a diptych page, the upper scent panel may run y=200–690 and the lower panel y=710–1200. Keep a strong boundary or 18–24 px breathing gap.

### Density

Hero and scent pages stay sparse. Technical pages may contain up to six short labels, three steps, or a 4-column matrix. Never combine a dual-scent diptych, formula factors, and pump operation on the same stage.

## Component Specifications

### CategorySignal

Optional opener using one verified rank or market statement. Include definition, period, geography, and source. Use black on porcelain or white on black. Keep product imagery small. Remove the component entirely if the context cannot withstand audit.

### SplitToneHero

Use a black-to-white field, preferably with a clean horizontal or vertical transition. Place one or two product variants at the seam so their contrast is visible. The claim sits fully in the dark zone in white, or fully in the light zone in black. One small variant label may bridge the zones.

### BeginnerPath

Frame the story as current state / simple intervention / attainable result. Use one relatable portrait or gesture rather than a collage of frustrations. Three short steps are enough. Avoid implying universal ease; state the intended skill level and required time if known.

### FinishComparison

Use matched before/after crops with the same subject, angle, lighting, and framing. Label finish characteristics such as shine, texture, volume, or definition. Do not alter one image more heavily than the other. If photography is illustrative, say so.

### DualScentWorld

Pair two equal material fields. Each world contains a name, 2–4 note labels, one mood sentence, and optionally one bottle crop. Use cool jade/mineral cues for one side and dark amber/wood cues for the other. Preserve parallel structure so the audience compares rather than merely browses.

### ScentDurationLedger

Present duration as a timeline or measured ledger, not a floating “24h” badge. State whether the number reflects instrumental testing, panel perception, or a claim reference. Show time points and qualifiers. If evidence is qualitative, use a fading intensity scale and label it as reported perception.

### FunctionMoisturePair

Explain two roles—such as styling and conditioning—without suggesting that one measured result proves both. Use a central cream specimen or product section, two labeled arrows, and one outcome per role. Ingredient names remain secondary to function.

### CreamSpecimen

Use a large macro smear on porcelain or black. Annotate pick-up, spread, residue, or finish with 2–3 concise labels. A subtle scale reference may appear. Avoid making the cream look like an unrelated food or cosmetic by keeping the product context nearby.

### PumpDoseDemo

Use a black title zone, a large light image field with a hand and pump, and a gray or porcelain note footer. Show lock/unlock, press, and dose if those are differentiators. A dose statement requires a unit or visual reference; “适量” may remain only when exact dosing is genuinely unnecessary.

### UseSequence

Three or four steps with stable portrait scale and one verb each. Sequence could be take / warm / distribute / shape. Put quantity and dry/wet condition at the relevant step. Use black dividers and white space instead of colorful numbered cards.

### WashabilityLedger

Use a neutral before/rinse/after test, with water condition and observation. Keep it separate from scent duration. One functional proof per ledger maintains credibility.

### ProductMatrix

Compare variants, finishes, fragrance worlds, or paired products across 3–5 explicit criteria. Keep portraits and products on consistent baselines. Use black rules and a single pale field for the recommendation. Avoid false precision scores.

### BrandProofStrip

Close with a narrow evidence strip containing only cleared certifications, milestones, or test standards. Equalize logo sizes. Provide one source line. It is a credibility footer, not a badge wall.

### IntegrityFooter

Required warnings, authenticity guidance, disclaimers, or legal notes receive a dedicated page or clearly separated footer. Use the 30px description level for actionable instructions and the 15px disclaimer level for formal details. High contrast is mandatory. Do not hide integrity content in decorative fine print.

## Page Blueprint Library

These blueprints maintain the source’s richness while assigning one communication job to each fixed stage.

### M01 — Split Variant Hero

- Place the claim fully in black or fully in white.
- Put one or two variants at the tonal seam with equal object scale.
- Identify the variant relationship in one short supporting line.
- Keep spectrum color out of the main background.
- Add status or awards only when they have complete provenance.

### M02 — Beginner Route

- Name the starting skill or routine honestly.
- Present one simple path in three stages.
- Use one relatable portrait or gesture per stage.
- State time, amount, or prerequisite when it changes the result.
- Finish with an attainable outcome rather than perfection language.

### M03 — Matched Finish

- Match subject, crop, angle, exposure, and retouching.
- Show one large pair instead of several small comparisons.
- Label only the finish dimensions the viewer can assess.
- Add an illustration note if capture conditions are not controlled.
- Keep performance numbers on a separate proof page.

### M04 — Cool Scent World

- Use a jade, mineral, water, or gray-stone material field.
- Include a world name, two to four notes, and one mood sentence.
- Place type in a calm, high-contrast area.
- Allow one small product anchor but no function grid.
- Establish the exact label structure that the warm world repeats.

### M05 — Warm Scent World

- Repeat the cool world’s scale and label positions.
- Change to wood, amber, resin, smoke, or deep shadow imagery.
- Preserve equal typographic authority between variants.
- Avoid romantic copy that overwhelms the actual note list.
- Do not add a duration badge unless method follows immediately.

### M06 — Function and Care

- Return to porcelain or white.
- Put a cream specimen or product section at the center.
- Explain styling and care as two distinct role paths.
- Give each path one supported outcome.
- Keep ingredient names smaller than functional explanation.

### M07 — Dose and Use

- Use a black headline band and a large light operation image.
- Show lock, press, amount, and application state as needed.
- Maintain product and hand scale across frames.
- Put conditions and cautions on the relevant step.
- End with the finished state, not a second sales claim.

### M08 — Evidence and Integrity

- Use one ledger for duration, removal, or status.
- Provide method and qualifiers beside the result.
- Keep brand proof to a narrow, rights-cleared strip.
- Give warnings or authenticity instructions a high-contrast zone.
- Close with an actionable verification or selection rule.

## Copy Architecture

### Variant line

Use `[shared category], two expressions: [cool world] / [warm world]`. Describe the difference in material and mood before introducing adjectives such as advanced or premium.

### Scent-world copy

Use `name + 2–4 notes + one mood sentence`. The mood sentence connects material to use context without claiming measurable psychological effects.

### Function pair

Use `[role A] supports [outcome A]; [role B] supports [outcome B]`. State evidence separately. Avoid one sentence that implies a shared formula automatically validates both outcomes.

### Duration statement

Use `[what persisted] + [time] + [assessment mode] + [qualifier]`. “24 hours” alone is not a complete content block.

### Integrity instruction

Use direct verbs, a visible route, and a confirmation state. Formal legal language may follow, but the user-facing action must remain understandable.

## Content and Evidence Discipline

Keep four narratives distinct:

1. Styling function: hold, finish, texture, control, ease.
2. Care function: moisture, conditioning, feel, removal.
3. Sensory world: scent, material association, mood, texture.
4. Evidence: tests, comparisons, status, standards, and sources.

A dual-function formula does not automatically prove a dual outcome. A fragrance note is not an ingredient benefit. A duration number must say what persisted and how it was assessed. A matched before/after needs comparable capture conditions. These distinctions should be visible in labels, not buried in notes.

## Image Direction

Use high-contrast bottle or tube cutouts, black-to-white gradients, matched hairstyle portraits, jade stone, gray mineral, seawater shadow, dark wood, amber resin, cream macro, pump-hand operation, and clean washability sequences. Favor editorial still life over busy lifestyle photography.

The cool and warm scent images should share framing logic but not color grading. Do not tint one generic stock image twice. Maintain real texture, scale, and material specificity.

## Depth and Effects

Most pages are flat editorial planes. Use the product shadow token for a central bottle or tube, not for every panel. On black, a subtle reflection or local glow may define a white product. On white, use natural contact shadow. The source contains many gradients, but they serve transitions and materials—not generic UI backgrounds.

Avoid neon cyan/red glow, chromatic aberration on body text, mirrored floors on every product, heavy grain, and excessive blur. Spectrum accents should read as a rare optical trace.

## Shapes and Rules

Use hard rectangles, full-bleed image bands, hairline dividers, circular pump details inside approved imagery, and occasional soft material contours. Every authored container has exactly square corners. The diptych depends on exact alignment. Avoid pill-shaped feature chips and oversized quote marks.

## Responsive, Presenter, Static Composition, and Print

Center and uniformly scale the complete `750×1320` stage. Presenter tools, notes, and navigation remain outside. On mobile, show complete stages in a vertical sequence rather than altering layouts.

Use fixed split-tone fields, matched before/after images, paired scent materials, and pump-operation frames. Keep one concept per page and establish sequence through placement, contrast, labels, and grouping. Measured product-duration timelines retain their stated time points and qualifiers as static evidence.

In print, verify shadow detail in black materials and separation between porcelain and paper white. Test all text on photographic material fields. Thin rules should remain at least 1.5 px at source size. Legal and source copy stays at the 15px disclaimer level.

## CJK Guidance

Use semantic breaks and plain functional verbs. Paired Chinese scent names should have equal optical scale even if lengths differ. Use the fixed -5% Chinese tracking. Attach Arabic numerals to units. Bilingual note names stay subordinate. Use a robust system CJK stack; the audited font is a reference, not an included asset.

## Iteration Guide

Begin with a seven-page backbone: hero, path, finish comparison, dual scent, function pair, pump/use, decision. Color-code the storyboard as black, white, cool material, or warm material. The sequence should visibly return to neutral after atmosphere.

Review in three passes. First, validate that each page communicates function, sensory world, or evidence—not a vague mixture. Second, compare paired photography for honest conditions and parallel structure. Third, inspect legal, duration, rank, and source text at actual export size. Only then tune gradients and static composition.
