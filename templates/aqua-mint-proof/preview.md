# Aqua Mint Proof — Preview

## Fixed-Brand Preview Override

The palette declared in this preview is the color authority for this option. Use its declared `light` or `dark` tone. Keep authored copy inside x=60–690 and y=120–1260. Chinese text uses only 75/45/45/30/15px, 100% line height, and -5% letter spacing. Every authored webpage layer and image mask uses `border-radius: 0`. Product-detail pages have no separately authored brand-logo layer. CSS visuals are allowed when selected; authorized images may be processed and rectangularly cropped while originals are preserved.

## Files

- `design.md` — complete visual system, component specifications, evidence rules, and fixed-stage behavior.
- `preview.md` — compact selection reference for choosing and applying this direction.

## Selection Metadata

**Best for:** cooling, hygiene, fragrance, personal-care, lightweight consumer science, packaging demonstrations, and product stories that must balance fresh sensation with verifiable evidence.

**Avoid for:** solemn financial reporting, heritage luxury, dense engineering schematics, or subjects that require warm human intimacy throughout.

**Source character:** a 750×15432 product-detail board with 12 sections, 93 text nodes, abundant product and water imagery, mostly white pages, mint evidence accents, and one immersive ocean-fragrance chapter.

**Fixed output:** every reusable page is a `750×1320` stage. Long source sections are interpreted and split; they are never squeezed into a single page.

## Palette Snapshot

- Frost `#F6FAF9`, paper `#FFFFFF`, ink `#111614`, copy `#525C58`, mist `#DDEEE8`, mint `#47AD8D`, deep mint `#2C856E`, ocean `#4173D1`, ice blue `#C9E4EE`, and line `#C9D9D4`.

## Visual Snapshot

Imagine an almost-white field with a faint mint temperature. A short black headline enters first; its opening phrase turns green. A large pale product silhouette rises through transparent glass ribbons and cool mist. Later, the deck opens into a full-bleed ocean horizon for fragrance, then snaps back to white for a measured result and source line.

The system should feel dry, cold, breathable, and precise. It should not feel like a hospital interface, a beverage campaign, or a default teal startup deck.

## Preview Ingredients

### Page 01 — Cooling promise

- Frost background with a restrained top-to-bottom mint cast.
- One `MintClaimTag`, maximum 10 Chinese characters.
- 75px black claim, no more than three lines.
- One large product crop and one translucent water or glass gesture.
- No chart, ingredient list, or small icon row.

### Page 02 — Benefit overview

- White editorial field.
- Four benefits as a rail or 2×2 band.
- Each item marked as experience, mechanism, or evidence where relevant.
- One application image or clean body-zone crop.
- Mint appears in titles or labels, not body paragraphs.

### Page 03 — Mechanism

- Simple before/action/after cutaway.
- Gray baseline, mint intervention, black outcome.
- One-sentence interpretation plus visible “示意图” label.
- Keep the diagram larger than its explanatory text.

### Page 04 — Scent horizon

- One full-bleed wave, horizon, condensation, or cool-water image.
- White or dark copy placed on a calm image region.
- Two to four scent notes, arranged with generous separation.
- No unrelated proof cards or commerce badges.

### Page 05 — Proof ledger

- Return to white.
- One large result, unit, and concise interpretation.
- Method, sample, time window, baseline, and source line.
- Optional compact chart with readable axes.

### Page 06 — Operation or choice

- Two or three product views demonstrating spray angle or direction.
- Thin operational arrows and concise captions.
- Or use a compact selector comparing up to four use situations.
- Close with one decision sentence, not another slogan.

## Text Richness Standard

Every page needs enough text to explain why the visual exists, but not enough to compete with it. A normal content page carries one claim, two to four supporting statements, and one source or qualifier. Body copy uses the 30px description level and should remain below roughly 85 Chinese characters per page. Evidence pages may use more metadata, provided the hierarchy stays obvious.

## Do / Don't Preview

**Do:** keep mint scarce; distinguish promise from proof; show quantitative method; use the ocean page once; return to white for credibility; preserve CJK line integrity; replace every source claim before reuse.

**Don't:** build a teal dashboard; use pale green body copy; cover every page with water; turn icons into evidence; omit units or qualifiers; reflow the 750×1320 stage; copy source logos, awards, fonts, or product data.

## CJK Note

Use a dependable Chinese system stack. Break headlines by meaning, attach numbers to units, and avoid one-character orphan lines. English fragrance or technical labels should be smaller than Chinese meaning. Proprietary source fonts are reference-only and are not included.

## Preview Rules

1. One dominant claim and one dominant image treatment per page.
2. Saturated mint occupies no more than about 2% of a normal light page.
3. A deck gets at most one immersive oceanic chapter break.
4. Every numeric proof keeps method, unit, sample, and time context.
5. All content remains inside the fixed `750×1320` stage and scales uniformly.
6. The reusable template contains no source-specific brand asset or unverified claim.
