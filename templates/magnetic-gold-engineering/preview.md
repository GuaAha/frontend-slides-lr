## Text Richness Standard

Dark pages stay terse: one claim, one qualifier, and one source or award line. Technical pages can carry 60–100 Chinese characters across labels and explanation, provided the object remains dominant. Every diagram label answers either “what is it,” “what does it do,” or “what should the user do.” Decorative specifications are removed.

## Do / Don't Preview

**Do:** alternate material and engineering pages; preserve real black; keep one light direction; verify rank and award provenance; maintain geometry across states; explain refill orientation; compare by user criteria; replace every source asset and claim.

**Don't:** make all pages black; set paragraphs in gold; fake metallic type; use impossible exploded views; substitute a magnet icon for operation; bury safety notes; create a dense commerce table; reflow the fixed stage.
